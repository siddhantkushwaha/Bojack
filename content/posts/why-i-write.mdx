I'm a backend engineer. I spend my days in code that has no UI, arguing about
consistency models and retry budgets. Writing publicly is not a natural act for
me. So why this?

Three reasons.

**Writing is thinking.** The fastest way I've found to discover that I *don't*
actually understand something is to try to explain it plainly. Half of these
posts started as a design doc I couldn't finish because a paragraph didn't hold
up.

**The good stuff is undocumented.** The interesting problems in backup,
storage, and distributed systems rarely make it into tidy tutorials. They live
in postmortems, tribal knowledge, and Slack threads. I want to write down a few
of them.

**Future me will thank present me.** I've solved the same class of problem more
than once because I never wrote down the reasoning the first time.

This blog stays intentionally minimal — no popups, no tracking theater, just
posts. If something here saves you an afternoon, that's the whole point.
